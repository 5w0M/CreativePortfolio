Capturing the facets of evolvability in a mechanistic framework
▼
Trends in Ecology & Evolution · 2022 · Co-author
  • Published in Trends in Ecology & Evolution (Impact factor: 20.6), one of the leading journals in evolutionary biology
  • Proposed a unifying framework to categorize evolvability determinants into three key categories: (1) determinants providing variation, (2) determinants shaping fitness effects, and (3) determinants shaping the selection process
  • Introduced critical distinctions between general vs. specific evolvability and broad vs. narrow scope of evolvability determinants
  • Co-authored with Jana M. Riederer, Timo J.B. van Eldijk, and Franz J. Weissing
  • Framework designed to reduce misunderstandings across disciplines and guide future evolutionary research

Reverse Evolution & Phenotypic Plasticity
▼
Evolutionary Biology · Peer-reviewed article
  • Dollo's law of irreversibility and plastic traits in parasitic wasps
  • Lipogenesis switches on in fat-poor environments and off in fat-rich environments
  • Lineages that lost lipogenic ability >200 million years ago still retain fat synthesis as a plastic trait
  • Individual-based simulations show plasticity can persist for thousands of generations

Behavioral Ecology · Field study
  • Automated RFID-surveillance system in wild field crickets (Gryllus campestris)
